# AI Workflow

1. Read root `MEMORY.md` and `AI_BOARD.md` before editing.
2. Declare one active identity: `frontend-ai`, `backend-ai`, `test-ai`, or `review-ai`.
3. Use `AI_BOARD.md` for open or blocked work. Do not create active boards under `.ai/docs/`.
4. Update `MEMORY.md` after material changes.
5. Keep stable, closed, or archival material under `.ai/docs/`.
6. Never commit private deployment secrets, passwords, production `.env` files, or local-only plans.

For cross-role changes, update the board before editing the dependent area.
